🍎 Foodie — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-16

Foodie is built to respect your privacy. In short: your food log never leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Foodie does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no logins, and no analytics or tracking.

Food search and barcode scanning

These two optional features are the only part of Foodie that uses the network. When you search for a food or scan a barcode, Foodie sends just that search text or barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free and open food database, to get calories and macros back.

Your food log, your calorie goal, and your history are never sent — not to Open Food Facts, and not to anyone else. Foodie sends no account, device, or advertising identifier with the request. Open Food Facts receives your device's IP address, as any website you visit would; see their privacy policy.

Many common foods are bundled inside the app, so those searches never touch the network at all. If you never search or scan, Foodie makes no network requests.

Camera

The camera is used only while the barcode scanner is open, and only to read a barcode on screen. No photo or video is recorded, saved, or transmitted.

Where your data lives

Everything you log — foods, calories, macros, and your goal — is stored locally on your device only. We do not have servers, and we cannot see your data. If you delete the app, the data is removed with it.

Backups you create

If you use the Export feature, a backup file is created and shared only to the destination you choose (e.g. Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop). That is entirely under your control.

Third parties

Foodie contains no advertising and no analytics. The only third-party service it contacts is Open Food Facts, and only for the searches and scans described above. Product data from Open Food Facts is © its contributors, licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL).

Children

Foodie does not collect data from anyone, including children.
